Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

This personal OAuth connection is used only by its owner to synchronize their own Google Drive files with their own computers. It is not offered to other users.

The connection requests Google Drive read/write authorization because it must access existing files. The local synchronization configuration targets a selected notes folder. Files travel between the owner’s computers and Google Drive; they are not published on this website, sold, or supplied to unrelated third parties by this connection.

Authorization credentials are stored in a protected local configuration on the owner’s server, outside the notes folder and coding-worker containers. The owner can revoke the connection through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings.

This information website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website access information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This site includes no analytics scripts or forms.
